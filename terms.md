# Signal QuickBooks Review
## End-User Licence Agreement

**Effective date:** 29 October 2026

### 1. Application owner

Signal QuickBooks Review is an internal business application operated by **Signal Fire and Security Limited**, Ireland.

### 2. Purpose

The application is designed to help authorised company personnel review QuickBooks Online accounting transactions, identify potential expense categorisation issues and receive recommendations for further investigation.

### 3. Authorised use

The application is intended solely for internal business use by Signal Fire and Security Limited and its authorised personnel.

It is not offered for sale, public distribution or use by unrelated third parties.

### 4. QuickBooks access

The application connects to QuickBooks Online using authorised access granted by the company.

Its intended accounting functionality is read-only. It may retrieve accounting information for analysis but does not create, modify, delete or automatically approve accounting transactions.

The application may perform necessary authentication and token-management operations to maintain authorised access.

### 5. Recommendations

Any categorisation recommendations are informational and must be reviewed by an authorised member of staff.

The application does not replace professional accounting advice or the company's established financial approval procedures.

### 6. Confidentiality

Users must protect confidential company information and must not disclose QuickBooks data, credentials or application access to unauthorised parties.

### 7. Availability and limitations

The application is provided for internal operational use. Its availability may depend on QuickBooks Online and third-party hosting and infrastructure providers.

Recommendations may be incomplete or inaccurate and should be independently verified before any accounting action is taken.

### 8. Changes

Signal Fire and Security Limited may update or discontinue the application or revise these terms as operational requirements change.

### 9. Contact

Questions concerning this application may be directed to Signal Fire and Security Limited through its established business contact channels.
