# Signal QuickBooks Review
## Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** 29 October 2026

### 1. Who we are

Signal QuickBooks Review is a private internal business application operated by **Signal Fire and Security Limited**, Ireland.

The application helps authorised company personnel analyse QuickBooks Online accounting information and identify potential expense categorisation issues.

### 2. Information processed

When authorised, the application may access accounting information from QuickBooks Online, including:

- Suppliers and customers associated with transactions
- Transaction dates, descriptions and amounts
- Expense categories and accounting references
- Billable expense indicators and customer associations
- Other transaction details necessary for categorisation analysis

Some accounting records may contain personal information.

### 3. How information is used

Information is processed to:

- Review accounting transactions
- Identify potential expense categorisation errors
- Generate recommendations for authorised personnel
- Investigate transactions requiring further verification
- Maintain the security and operation of the application

The application is not intended to make automatic changes to accounting records.

### 4. QuickBooks authorisation

The application connects to QuickBooks Online using Intuit's authorised authentication process.

Access credentials and tokens must be protected against unauthorised disclosure.

Authentication tokens may be securely stored and refreshed to maintain authorised access.

### 5. Hosting and service providers

The application uses third-party infrastructure, including:

- **Render**, for hosting the application
- **Supabase**, for application data storage and authentication-token management
- **Intuit QuickBooks Online**, as the accounting data source

Where ChatGPT is used to review retrieved information, relevant information may also be processed by OpenAI under the applicable service terms and data controls.

### 6. Access and security

Access to the application is intended to be restricted to authorised personnel.

Security measures include application authentication, encrypted storage of authentication tokens and restrictions on accounting write operations.

No internet-connected system can be guaranteed completely secure.

### 7. Data retention

Accounting information is accessed for internal review purposes.

Retention of application data, operational logs and authentication records is governed by the company's applicable retention practices and the configurations of its service providers.

Information should not be retained longer than necessary for the stated purposes or applicable legal obligations.

### 8. Data sharing

Accounting information is not intended to be sold or disclosed for advertising purposes.

Information may be processed by the service providers necessary to operate the application, subject to their applicable terms and data-protection arrangements.

### 9. Data protection rights

Individuals whose personal information is processed may have rights under applicable data-protection law, including the GDPR, subject to relevant legal exceptions.

Requests concerning personal information may be directed to Signal Fire and Security Limited through its established business contact channels.

### 10. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the application's functionality, service providers or data-handling practices change.
